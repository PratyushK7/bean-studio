== DESCRIPTION ==
How many mosquitoes would it take to drain all your blood? Roughly a million.
How much blood does a mosquito drink? A drop smaller than a grain of rice, and the human body holds about five litres.

An adult has about 5 litres of blood. A mosquito takes roughly 1 to 10 microlitres per bite, so at about 5 microlitres each, you'd need around a million bites to empty you. Only females bite. Danger comes much sooner: losing about 40% of your blood is life-threatening, which is roughly 400,000 bites. You'd swat long before that. 🫘
