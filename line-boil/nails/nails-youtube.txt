== DESCRIPTION ==
What happens if you don't cut your nails? They keep growing, curl into spirals and get in the way of everything.
How fast do fingernails grow? About 3.5 mm a month, so one year adds roughly half a finger.

A root under your skin, the nail matrix, keeps making new cells. They fill with keratin, the same protein as hair, die, and get pushed forward. Left alone, long nails twist and turn brittle. If they never broke, a lifetime of growth could make each one longer than you are tall. One man grew the nails on his left hand for 66 years. They reached 909.6 cm combined, and the hand can no longer straighten or move its fingers.
