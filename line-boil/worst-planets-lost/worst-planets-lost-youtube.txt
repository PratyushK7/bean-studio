== DESCRIPTION ==
Which planet is the worst one to get lost on? We rank Mercury, Jupiter, Neptune and Venus, from bad to worst.
A stranded bean, four planets, and the numbers NASA actually publishes: roughly 800 F days, no ground, 1,200 mph winds, and a surface hot enough to melt lead.

The short answer: Venus is the worst. Its ground is about 872 F (467 C), the air presses about 93 times harder than on Earth (like being roughly 3,000 feet underwater), and the clouds are sulfuric acid. Mercury swings from about 800 F by day to about -290 F at night. Jupiter has no surface at all. Neptune has no surface either, and the solar system's fastest winds, over 1,200 mph. The ranking itself is a joke; the numbers are not.
