## Not claimed, on purpose
- Which planet is "worst" is a joke ranking, an opinion, and the script says so by its framing ("rank").
- No claim that Mercury has literally no air at all: the script says "no real atmosphere", and "no air to complain about it" is a joke on the thin exosphere.
- No Mars, Saturn or Uranus numbers: not checked, so left out.
- "Getting denser as you sink" on Jupiter is the standard simplification of pressure rising with depth; no depth figures are given.
- Rounded: 872 F said as "about eight hundred seventy"; 93 times as "ninety-three"; 930 m as "a kilometer".
