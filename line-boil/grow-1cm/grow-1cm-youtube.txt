== DESCRIPTION ==
What if you grew one centimetre every single second? Here is how tall you would be after a minute, an hour, a day and a few months, and what would go wrong first.
A centimetre a second is about 36 metres an hour. Your bones would give out long before the Moon.

The short answer: at one centimetre a second you pass the tallest human on record (Robert Wadlow, 2.72 m) in under two minutes, pass the Burj Khalifa (828 m) in under a day and Everest (8,849 m) in about ten days. The catch is that double your height and your weight goes up eight times while your bones get only about four times stronger, so past roughly three metres you would probably collapse. Pretend your body keeps up and your head reaches the edge of space (100 km) in about four months and the Moon (384,400 km) in roughly twelve hundred years. Your trousers would not keep up.
