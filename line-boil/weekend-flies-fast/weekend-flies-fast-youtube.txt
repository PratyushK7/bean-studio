== DESCRIPTION ==
Why does the weekend feel like it lasts 10 minutes, when Monday drags on forever? Scientists think it comes down to how often you check the clock.
Your brain has no clock you can read, so it guesses time by how much attention you give to it. Here is why the weekend flies by.

A weekend is 48 hours, and a Monday meeting is made of the exact same minutes. Scientists think your brain estimates time partly by how much attention you pay to time itself. At your desk you keep checking the clock, so time feels long. On a Saturday you are busy, you forget to look, and time feels shorter. The real clock never changes speed.
