# Voice: The Bean With Bad Ideas

Inferred from the channel's first two Shorts, "What If Your Sweat Was Made of Coca-Cola?" and "How Many
Balloons Would It Take to Lift You Into the Sky?" (both scripted with the line-boil skill, November 2026).

## What the channel is
"The Bean With Bad Ideas" is the NAME, not a limit. It is a funny hand-drawn explainer channel about anything that helps people
learn something: science, health, money, tech, history, psychology, how things work, everyday life. Pick topics for what the
viewer will learn and be able to repeat to a friend, not for a bad-idea angle. A bad idea is one device among many.

## Who it's for
Curious general viewers, teens to adults, on Shorts and long videos. No background assumed in any subject.

## How it sounds
- Narrated by a calm, friendly voice (Kokoro af_heart), not a hyped presenter.
- Opens on a question or a claim the viewer half-believes, asked plainly: "What if...", "How many...", "Why does...", "Is it true that...".
- Short sentences, one idea each. Second person: "you", "your".
- Turns on "But..." to deliver the real science.
- Dry, understated humour in every genre. The joke is in the image and the last line, not in exclamation marks. Funny never replaces correct.
- Ends on a punchy callback: "Salty water keeps you cool, not candied." / "Getting down is the hard part."

## Words it uses
what if, roughly, about, nearly, probably, turns out, here's the catch, bad idea (sparingly), sticky, swell, pop.

## Words and moves it avoids
- Hype: "INSANE", "you won't believe", "mind-blowing", all-caps shouting.
- Clickbait it can't back up. Every number is checked and rounded honestly ("roughly seven thousand").
- Jargon without a picture (no "buoyancy" or "Rayleigh scattering" on their own).
- Emoji walls. One or two at most (🫘💡 are the channel's own).

## What it won't claim
Hedges anything uncertain ("probably", "can", "scientists think"). Simplifies, never fakes.
