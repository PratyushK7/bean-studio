# Scene and sound plan: How Does Caffeine Steal Your Sleep?

**The metaphor (one for the whole video):** tiredness is a sleepy mail carrier bean delivering letters that say TIRED to the brain's
mailboxes (adenosine receptors). Caffeine is a small, wired red bean that squeezes into the mailbox slot wearing a CLOSED sign, so the
letters pile up outside. The liver is the slow bean who escorts caffeine out, half at a time. The opening image (a washed mug and a
clock at 3 p.m.) and the CLOSED sign come back in the last two lines.

**Cast** (`web/cast.js`), one bean per role, different at a glance:

| Bean | Body | Costume | Wants | Habit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| YOU (the viewer) | `bean`, PAPER | blue beanie with a yellow pom (doubles as a nightcap), red cheeks, a red mug | to feel fine and sleep | deadpan, then a slow take |
| TIREDNESS (adenosine, the mail carrier) | `wide`, BLUE | red postal cap, yellow mail bag, letters that say TIRED | to deliver every letter | always half-asleep, never hurries |
| CAFFEINE | `ball`, RED, small | spiky black hair, huge eyes, a CLOSED sign on a string | to sit in the slot | wired, smug, never blinks |
| LIVER | `pear`, YEL | blue top hat with a red band, glasses, a pocket clock, a LIVER label | to clear caffeine, slowly | fussy, patient, sighs |
| SCIENTIST (Drake's team) | `tall`, RED | glasses, paper lab coat (belly), tuft, clipboard | to measure it | nods, unimpressed |
| SLEEPERS | `tiny`, PAPER and BLUE | headbands, in a row of beds | sleep | snore, then don't |

Props are props: the mailbox (a face under the slot), envelopes with sleepy faces, a wall clock, a mug, beds, a sleep monitor screen,
a stack of papers, a dumbbell, label shapes for the numbers. Burned-in captions are OFF; numbers and key words are label shapes.
