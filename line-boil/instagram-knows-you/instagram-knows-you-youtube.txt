== DESCRIPTION ==
How does Instagram know what you want? It isn't listening through your microphone, Instagram says. It's keeping score.

Instagram says it has no single algorithm. Feed, Reels and Explore weigh what you like, save, share and comment on, how likely you are to linger on a post, and what lots of people are loving right now. Ads can also use shopping on other sites, if those sites share it with Meta. So it isn't a mind reader, just a very patient note-taker. 🫘
