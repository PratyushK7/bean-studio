# Sources: How Instagram Knows Exactly What You Want

| Claim in the script | Source | Checked |
|---|---|---|
| "Instagram says no" to listening through the microphone | Adam Mosseri's video denial (reported Oct 2025): https://storyboard18.com/digital/instagram-ceo-adam-mosseri-denies-using-phone-microphones-to-eavesdrop-on-users-81974.htm and https://hothardware.com/news/is-instagram-eavesdropping-ceo-speaks-out | secondary reports only (not the original video) |
| Likes, shares, saves and comments are signals ("keeping score") | Instagram, "Instagram Ranking Explained": https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained ("Posts you've liked, shared, saved or commented on...") | official page, read via fetch |
| It predicts whether you'll linger ("spend a few seconds on a post") | Same page: Feed predicts how likely you are to spend a few seconds on a post | official page |
| It spots what lots of people are loving now and tests it on you | Same page: Explore ranks by how many people like, comment, share and save a post and how quickly, "much more" than in Feed or Stories | official page |
| Ads can use shopping on other sites, if those sites share it | Mosseri's explanation in the same coverage: advertiser-shared website visits | secondary reports only |

## Not claimed, on purpose
- That Instagram "never" listens: only that Instagram says it does not.
- That time spent is a direct ranking input: the official page does not say so; the script only says it predicts lingering.
- Any numbers, weights or an exact order of signals. Instagram says there is no single algorithm.
- Buffer's 2026 "top signals" list (watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach): could not be confirmed against Mosseri's original.
