| Claim in the script | Source | Checked |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram says it does not listen (microphone) | Adam Mosseri's video, early Oct 2025: https://hothardware.com/news/is-instagram-eavesdropping-ceo-speaks-out , https://storyboard18.com/digital/instagram-ceo-adam-mosseri-denies-using-phone-microphones-to-eavesdrop-on-users-81974.htm | secondary reports (several outlets agree); original video not watched |
| Mosseri's argument: constant recording would drain the battery and trigger the microphone indicator | Same coverage (search summary of multiple outlets) | secondary reports only |
| Likes, shares, saves and comments are signals of interest | Instagram, "Instagram Ranking Explained": https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained ("Posts you've liked, shared, saved or commented on help us understand what you might be interested in.") | official page, full text fetched |

| Advertisers can share websites you visited | Mosseri's explanation (advertiser data from website visits) in the same coverage | secondary reports only |
| People forget the ads they scrolled past and remember the one that matched | Mosseri's stated reason ("people forgetting ads they scrolled past") in the same coverage | secondary reports only |

## Not claimed, on purpose
- That Instagram "never" listens: only that Instagram says it does not. The script says "Instagram says no".
- Any numbers, weights or an order of signals. The "a hundred ads" and "ten minutes later" are storytelling, not data.
- Meta's AI-chat ad personalisation (from Dec 2025): a different data source, left out to keep this short.
- That it has been proven independently that no app ever listens.
