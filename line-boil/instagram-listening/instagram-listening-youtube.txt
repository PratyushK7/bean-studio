== DESCRIPTION ==
Does Instagram actually listen to you through your microphone? Instagram says no, and here is what it does instead.

Instagram's boss, Adam Mosseri, said in 2025 that secretly recording everyone would drain your battery and light up your microphone indicator. The app doesn't need to: it learns from your likes, saves, shares and comments, guesses whether you'll stop and stare at a post, and tests what lots of people are loving right now. Advertisers can also share which of their websites you visited, and we forget the ads we scrolled past but remember the one that matched our chat. So it isn't a mind reader, just a very patient note-taker. 🫘
