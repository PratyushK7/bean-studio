Tracks (library as of 2026-11-17): At Rest, Bittersweet, Comfortable Mystery 4, Dark Fog, Disquiet, Dreamy Flashback, Eternal Hope, Healing,
Heart of Nowhere, Heartwarming, Hidden Past, Inspired, Investigations, Lightless Dawn, Long Note Two, Ossuary 6 - Air, Unanswered Questions.

`_analysis.json` holds each track's measured loudness, tempo estimate and how much it swells; `_loudness.json` is the renderer's level cache.
Moods were assigned from the track names, what Kevin MacLeod's catalogue says about them and these measurements; nobody has auditioned them here.
