# The three reference documentaries: how they hold an audience

Studied line by line from their timestamped captions (fetched 2026-11-19 with `scripts/timed_transcript.py`; the files are in
`channels/jadtak/research/reference-transcripts/`). All three tell the same life (Neem Karoli Baba), which makes the
differences in craft easy to see. They are **models for structure and rhythm**, and **secondary research sources** (their
claims are logged in `reference-claims.md`). They are not text to copy: Jad Tak never reuses their sentences, scenes or
invented dialogue.

minute than the 11-minute video, yet it starts a new anecdote every 1:45 and never goes two minutes without a turn.

## 4. Cross-video patterns worth copying

1. **Start inside a story, not with an introduction.** All three open on an event with a date or a scene; none opens with
   "आज हम बात करेंगे". The formal promise comes after the first story (1:00, 3:01, 3:56).
2. **The cold open ends at the subject's fame or its mystery.** SM's opening story ends with thousands arriving at Kainchi;
   AG's ends with a station built for a sadhu; RZ's ends with "एक संत जिसका अवतार माना जाता है".
3. **Three promises, then the name.** Escalating parallel lines (RZ) or rapid questions (SM), followed by the name and title.
4. **Each anecdote is complete and ends on its strangest fact.** Setup, a want or worry, the unusual act, a pause, the reveal
   as the last line, then one line of meaning.
5. **Bridges carry a promise.** Sections hand over with "the first witness was...", "and here he met a man who would...",
   or a date stamp. No "अब बात करते हैं".
6. **Explain once, in one breath.** Teleportation, Kriya Yoga, the name Kainchi: each in a single sentence with an image.
7. **Order: miracle-led or outsider-led beats chronology.** RZ goes miracle → miracle → outsider → death. SM goes outsider →
   life in order → outsiders again. AG goes miracle → life in order with miracles placed for escalation → outsiders → end →
   legacy → callback.
8. **The death is a chapter of its own** with a contradiction and a belief/record pair; the legacy follows with a near-miss.
9. **Endings return to the start.** AG's last scene redefines the cold open. RZ ends on "आज भी". SM ends on a method note.
10. **CTA only at the end**, and it asks about the viewer's own experience.

## 5. What not to copy

| Problem | Where | Jad Tak rule |
|---|---|---|
| Invented dialogue and names in scenes presented as the story | AG throughout (e.g. a couple named in the film at 14:31; Jobs and Zuckerberg's conversation at 35:11 to 35:50) | Never script dialogue a source does not record. Tell the scene in narration; quote only recorded words with provenance. |
| Factual slips | SM 00:00 dates the Harvard dismissal to "1950 के दशक"; SM 02:05 gives doses in "मिलीग्राम"; RZ 07:42 says both books were written while staying with the Baba; RZ 08:09 says his last day was at Kainchi | Every date, unit and place goes through the ledger; reference videos are leads, not proof. |
| Contradictions between videos told as fact | Kainchi "20 km" (SM 01:09) vs "17 km" (AG 20:44); barber's son "100 km" (RZ 02:49) vs "100 मील" (AG 14:09); Zuckerberg "2004 to 05" (SM 17:45) vs "around 2008", "about a month" (AG 34:50, 35:54) | Log both; use the corroborated one, or round honestly ("कुछ किलोमीटर दूर") or leave it out. |
| Unattributed sweeping claims | RZ 00:49 "किसी भी बीमारी को ठीक करने की शक्ति थी" | A believer's claim is said as theirs: "भक्त मानते हैं ...". |
| Misattributed quotations | AG 34:31 puts a famous advertising line in Jobs's India story | Quote only with the exact source. |
| The sponsor in the hook zone | RZ 01:14 to 02:13 | No ad read, promo or digression in the first two minutes. |
| An opened loop never closed | SM 09:47 (the blanket stories) | Every mentioned story is told or not mentioned. |
| Too much English | SM ("इन्फ्लुएंशियल स्पिरिचुअल ग्रुप", "एग्जीक्यूट करने की क्लेरिटी") | Everyday Hindi first (see hindi-narration.md). |
| Rushed famous names | RZ 07:59 (four names in one sentence, no detail) | One developed story beats four names. |
