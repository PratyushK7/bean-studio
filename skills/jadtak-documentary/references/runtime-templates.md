# Runtime templates

Three blueprints derived from the reference videos (`transcript-analysis.md`, section 6). Times are targets, not cages: move
a beat by up to a third of its window when the story needs it, never drop one without replacing its job.

**Narration rate for planning: 160 Hindi words a minute** (Jad Tak's calm narrator; the references run 180 to 195 for pure
narration). Word budgets below use 160. The scratch voice (Kokoro) runs nearer 145 with pauses, so check the real runtime
from the scratch narration and trim or add one anecdote, never pad.

| Band | Target | Words | Anecdotes told in full | Chapters | Something new | Re-hook at least every |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short | 10 to 12 min | 1,600 to 1,900 | 4 to 5 | 4 to 5 (no acts) | every 15 to 25 s | 60 s |
| Standard | 18 to 22 min | 2,900 to 3,500 | 7 to 10 | 6 to 8 | every 20 to 30 s | 90 s |
| Long | 35 to 40 min | 5,600 to 6,400 | 14 to 20 | 9 to 12, in 5 to 7 acts | every 30 to 45 s | 120 s, and at every act seam |

"Something new" means one of: a fact the viewer did not know, a turn, a reveal, a consequence, a new person, a new place. A
re-hook is a line that makes the next minute necessary: an open question, a foreshadow, a contradiction, a withheld detail,
a "but". See `hook-library.md` for the lines.

Every template ends with the same three jobs: answer the central question honestly, call back to the opening, ask the
viewer one question. CTA lines live only there (see `hindi-narration.md`, "Audience address").

## A. Short: 10 to 12 minutes (target 11:00, about 1,750 words)

Model: RZ (four stories, one death question, fast transitions). Fast, story-to-story, one central question.

| Time | Words | Beat | Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:05 | 10 | **First line** | A date-place stamp, or the single strangest true image of the story. No greeting, no "आज हम". |
| 0:05 to 0:30 | 60 | **Compressed origin or micro-story** | Either an ordinary life in 4 to 6 sentences ending on a turn sentence (D2), or a 25-second anecdote that ends on its contradiction (D7). |
| 0:30 to 0:55 | 65 | **Promise stack** | Three parallel lines, each a bigger true claim (D3). The last one carries the central question's subject. |
| 0:55 to 1:05 | 20 | **Name + title** | The name alone as a sentence (D4). Title sting. |
| 1:05 to 1:25 | 50 | **Frame + central question** | "जब भी ... नाम आता है ..." and the question in one plain sentence. |
| 1:25 to 3:30 | 330 | **Story 1: the strongest clean anecdote** | Full anecdote engine (setup → want → unusual act → pause → punchline reveal → meaning). One-breath explainer if the story needs a concept. Re-hook at its end. |
| 3:30 to 3:45 | 40 | **Bridge** | A foreshadow bridge (D13) or "पहला गवाह" line into the origin. |
| 3:45 to 5:45 | 320 | **Story 2: origin / first witness** | Where the reputation or the conflict began. End on the consequence (crowd reaction, D24). |
| 5:45 to 6:00 | 40 | **Bridge** | Time jump (D26) or "और इसी जगह ..." |
| 6:00 to 7:45 | 280 | **Story 3: the outsider or famous connection** | Outsider's credentials and doubt first (D5), the test, the turn, the delayed name if famous (D4). |
| 7:45 to 8:10 | 65 | **Wider consequence** | What changed because of story 3. A montage only if each name is sourced (D15). Re-hook into the final chapter. |
| 8:10 to 10:00 | 290 | **Story 4: the turning point or the ending** | Date stamp; contradiction + question (D7); the detailed chain of events; belief/record pair (D18). |
| 10:00 to 10:35 | 90 | **Present day + answer** | "आज भी ..." (D20). Answer the central question in two sentences: what the record shows, what belief holds. |
| 10:35 to 10:50 | 40 | **Callback** | Return to the first image with its new meaning (D17). |
| 10:50 to 11:00 | 30 | **CTA** | One call to action: the next Jad Tak video (hook, curiosity, action). |

Rules for this band: transitions are one sentence; no chapter recaps; no method note longer than one sentence (place it
inside the answer); at most one concept explainer per story.

## B. Standard: 18 to 22 minutes (target 20:00, about 3,200 words)

Model: SM's shape (a complete outsider story as the cold open, then the life) with AG's density of personal stories.

| Time | Words | Beat | Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 2:45 | 440 | **Multi-layered cold open** | One complete story that is not the beginning of the life: an outsider, a stranger, an unexpected connection (D5, D6). It has its own setup, secret, test and turn, and it ends at the subject's fame or central mystery. The subject's name lands by 0:50 (D4). |
| 2:45 to 3:15 | 80 | **Rapid-fire promise** | Three or four questions, escalating, each answered later in the video (D3 as questions). Close with one line that names what the viewer gets (a true story, its strangest episode, the honest answer). |
| 3:15 to 3:25 | 25 | **Title** | |
| 3:25 to 6:30 | 490 | **Ch 1: Where it began** | Date-place stamp. The family and the first sign that this person is different, told through one or two short personal vignettes, not a list. End on the first irreversible decision. |
| 6:30 to 9:00 | 400 | **Ch 2: The first story people told** | The first public anecdote, fully developed. A name-origin or place-origin mini-mystery (D23) if available. Re-hook. |
| 9:00 to 11:30 | 400 | **Ch 3: The cost** | A personal turn with an emotional price (a return home, a loss, a sacrifice). Then the founding of the place or work the video is about, with a one-breath explainer (D8). |
| 11:30 to 14:00 | 400 | **Ch 4: The world arrives** | Callback to the cold open (D17), then a second outsider story that escalates the first. |
| 14:00 to 16:00 | 320 | **Ch 5: What made it different** | Character by negation (D14) plus one conflict scene with a sourced quote. |
| 16:00 to 18:15 | 360 | **Ch 6: The ending** | Foreboding signs (only if sourced), the dated chain of events, the belief/record pair (D18). |
| 18:15 to 19:30 | 200 | **Ch 7: Legacy** | The poignant near-miss (D16), one or two modern connections with sources, the present day (D20). |
| 19:30 to 19:50 | 55 | **Payoff** | Answer the central question; the one sentence worth remembering. |
| 19:50 to 20:00 | 30 | **Callback + CTA** | The cold open's image with its new meaning; one call to action to the next video. |

Rules: every chapter seam has a re-hook (the last line of a chapter opens the next); one mid-chapter re-hook every ~90 s;
re-orient with a date stamp when time jumps; one method note, at most two sentences, in the payoff.

## C. Long: 35 to 40 minutes (target 38:00, about 6,000 words)

Model: AG's architecture (a staged mystery cold open, anecdote chains escalating by stakes, a seeded maxim paid off later,
outsiders with delayed names, a callback ending) told by a narrator, without invented dialogue.

Choose one spine and say it in the outline:
- **Thematic acts** (default): each act answers one question about the subject.
- **Numbered revelations**: the promise names a number ("इस कहानी में पाँच ऐसे मोड़ हैं ...") and each act is one
  revelation. Use only when the material genuinely has that many distinct turns; number them on screen.

| Time | Words | Beat | Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 3:30 | 520 | **Mystery cold open** | The most visual, most self-contained anecdote, staged in narration with a withheld element (D10) and a punchline reveal (D11). It must contain an image the ending can return to. |
| 3:30 to 4:15 | 120 | **Promise + title** | The central question and the spine (thematic or numbered), plus one line of stakes: why this question still matters. |
| 4:15 to 9:00 | 760 | **Act 1: Origins** | 3 or 4 short vignettes (30 to 60 s each). Seed one maxim or object that pays off in Act 4 (D12 + D13). Dramatic irony line at the end (D13). |
| 9:00 to 13:30 | 720 | **Act 2: The hidden years** | The emotional act: leaving, returning, a family's price. Slower cuts. One sourced quote at most. End on the decision that sets the public life in motion. |
| 13:30 to 20:00 | 1,040 | **Act 3: The stories people tell** | Three anecdotes escalating by stakes: personal → life and death → public. Each ends on its reveal; one maxim after the biggest; each gets one natural attribution at entry. |
| 20:00 to 24:30 | 720 | **Act 4: The place** | Founding story with a one-breath explainer of the place; the public-scale payoff that closes the Act 1 seed; present day (D20). |
| 24:30 to 30:30 | 960 | **Act 5: Outsiders** | Two or three outsider stories, each with a delayed identity (D4) or a future-consequence reveal (D16). Order by surprise, not chronology. |
| 30:30 to 33:30 | 480 | **Act 6: The ending** | Sourced foreboding, the dated chain, the belief/record pair, a callback line to an Act 3 miracle (D17). |
| 33:30 to 36:30 | 480 | **Act 7: Legacy** | Near-miss story (D16), modern connections, then "अब ज़रा रुककर सोचिए" (D19). |
| 36:30 to 37:45 | 200 | **Resolution** | The honest answer to the central question; the method note in two sentences (D22); the callback that gives the cold open its new meaning (D17). |
| 37:45 to 38:00 | 40 | **Closing line + CTA** | One closing line in the narrator's words, then one call to action to the next video. |

Rules: each act opens with a date-place stamp or a time jump and a one-line re-orientation ("अब तक हमने देखा ... अब ..."
only at act seams, never inside an act); each act ends with a handoff line; no more than two acts in a row with the same
rhythm (alternate story-heavy and explanation-heavy); 3 to 5 narrator maxims in the whole video; 12 to 20 visual
"chapter" title cards.

## Checking a plan against its band

Before writing, fill this for the chosen band in `<slug>-plan.md`:

| Check | Short | Standard | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| First complete anecdote starts by | 1:30 | 0:05 (the cold open) | 0:05 |
| Name revealed by | 1:05 | 0:50 | 4:15 |
| Promise delivered (every promised item paid) | yes | yes | yes |
| Anecdotes in full | 4 to 5 | 7 to 10 | 14 to 20 |
| Longest stretch without something new | 25 s | 30 s | 45 s |
| Longest stretch without a re-hook | 60 s | 90 s | 120 s |
| Open loops at once (excluding the central question) | ≤ 2 | ≤ 3 | ≤ 4 |
| Callback to the opening in the last 5% | yes | yes | yes |
